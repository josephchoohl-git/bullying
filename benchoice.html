What could Ben do instead?

Ben wanted his friends to laugh and to feel liked.

Let’s think

  • What did Ben really want?
  • What is one kind way to get it?
  • What could he do to make things right with Mei Ling?

What Ben chose

Ben said sorry to Mei Ling. He asked her to draw a football poster for the class. His friends loved it, and now Ben and Mei Ling both feel proud.

Kind way, same wish :)

About 4 minutes. Show the left side first. In Present mode, the yellow note fades in on the next click, so the students can come up with their own ideas before seeing Ben’s choice. Draw out their ideas first and praise any kind option. Then reveal what Ben chose. Point out that Ben still got what he wanted (laughter, friends, feeling important) but in a kind way, and that he “made it right” by saying sorry and doing something kind. This leads into Part 3.
