Part 1 · What is bullying?

One week at recess

Monday

Ben calls Mei Ling’s drawing a “baby drawing”. His friends laugh.

Tuesday

Ben grabs her drawing book and shows it around.

Wednesday

Ben tells the others, “Don’t sit with her.”

Thursday

Mei Ling says, “Stop it.” Ben laughs and does it again.

Is this a joke, a fight or bullying?

You vote!

About 3 minutes. Read each day aloud, or ask the students to take turns reading. Then ask the question on the slide and let them vote: joke, fight or bullying? Don’t correct yet: ask “What makes you think so?” Common answer: “It was just a joke.” Reply with curiosity: “Was Mei Ling laughing too?” The next slide gives the three clues to check.
