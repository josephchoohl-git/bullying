SESSION SET-UP (for the counsellor). Audience: 2 P3 students, small-group psychoeducation, about 40 minutes. Purpose: build understanding of bullying, empathy and kinder choices WITHOUT labelling or penalising the two students. Throughout, talk about behaviour, not people: say “hurtful actions” or “the person who did the hurting”, never “bully”. The story characters (Ben, Mei Ling, Hari) give the students a safe distance: let them talk about Ben rather than themselves. If they make links to their own behaviour on their own, welcome it calmly (“Thank you for being honest. That takes courage.”). Optional framing: invite them to be your “helpers” today, learning this so they can help make recess kinder for everyone. Keep it warm, curious and light.
