Part 1 · What is bullying?

Meet three classmates

B

Ben

Loves football. Likes making his friends laugh.

M

Mei Ling

Loves drawing. Quiet and gentle.

H

Hari

Sits near them in class. Sees what happens at recess.

About 2 minutes. Introduce the three P3 classmates as ordinary kids. Stress that Ben is not a “bad boy”: he is fun and likes football. Ask: “Which one is most like someone in your class?” Accept any answer. These three characters return in every part of the lesson, so the students can talk about Ben’s choices without having to talk about their own.
