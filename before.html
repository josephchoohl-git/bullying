Before we start

No one is in trouble today

We are here to learn together, like a team.

Listen

One person talks at a time.

Be honest

There are no wrong answers here.

Be kind

We use kind words with each other.

Thumbs up if you agree!

About 2 minutes. Say clearly and warmly: “You are not in trouble. Today we’re learning about something important that happens in many schools.” This lowers defensiveness so they can think instead of protect themselves. Ask each student to agree to the three rules with a thumbs up. If useful, add: “I chose you two because I think you can help make our school kinder.” Keep the tone curious, not corrective.
