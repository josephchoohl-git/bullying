Part 2 · How does bullying affect me and others?

What happens to Ben?

At first

His friends laugh. He feels big and important.

then…

Later

  • Classmates stop trusting him.
  • Mei Ling stays away from him.
  • His teacher and parents are worried.
  • Deep inside, he does not feel good.

Hurting others ends up hurting me too.

About 3 minutes. This is the key slide for this group, so go gently. Ask: “Ben’s friends laughed. Was Ben happy at the end of the week? Why not?” Help them see the cost to Ben himself: lost trust, fewer real friends, adults worried, an uncomfortable feeling inside. Avoid sounding like a warning or punishment. The message is that Ben is a good kid who made hurtful choices, and choices can change. If a student says “That’s like me”, respond warmly: “Thank you for being honest. That shows you’re ready to make a different choice.”
