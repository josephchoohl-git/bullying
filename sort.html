Part 1 · Let’s try

Fun or hurtful? You decide

Ask: Is everyone having fun? Did someone say stop?

A

Wei Jie and Ravi joke about each other. Both laugh. When Ravi says “enough”, Wei Jie stops.

B

Every day, a classmate hides Aisyah’s pencil case and laughs at her.

C

Two friends argue once over a game. The next day, they play together again.

D

Some classmates keep sending “nobody likes you” to one girl in the class chat.

About 4 minutes. Each student points or holds up a thumb (up = fun, down = hurtful). Answers: A is fun teasing between friends: both enjoy it, and it stops when asked. B is bullying: it hurts, it happens every day, and Aisyah cannot stop it. C is a one-time disagreement, not bullying, though it still helps to talk it out. D is bullying online: hurtful words, again and again. Praise good reasoning more than right answers: “I like how you checked whether everyone was having fun.”
