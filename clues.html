Part 1 · What is bullying?

Bullying has three clues

When all three clues are there, it is bullying.

1

It hurts

Someone feels hurt, even if it was “just a joke”.

2

Again and again

It keeps happening, not just once.

3

Hard to stop

The person being hurt cannot make it stop alone.

About 3 minutes. Go back to Mei Ling’s week and check each clue together. 1: Did it hurt? (Yes, she was not laughing.) 2: Did it happen again and again? (Monday to Thursday.) 3: Could she make it stop? (She said “Stop it” and it continued.) So yes, it was bullying. Key message for this group: whether something is a joke depends on how the OTHER person feels, not on what the person doing it meant. Keep the focus on the actions, not on Ben as a person.
