Part 2 · Why do people do hurtful things?

We all want the same things

There is a hurtful way and a kind way to get each one.

I want to…

Hurtful way

Kind way

Feel important

Laugh at someone

Help someone, or show what I’m good at

Have friends

Leave someone out

Invite someone to join us

Have fun

Tease or trick someone

Play a game everyone enjoys

About 4 minutes. This slide uses Choice Theory needs in child-friendly words: feel important (power), have friends (love and belonging), have fun (fun). Message: wanting these things is normal and good. The question is HOW we get them. Ask: “What do you think Ben wanted when he laughed at Mei Ling’s drawing?” (To feel important, to make his friends laugh.) Then: “Which of these do YOU like most: feeling important, having friends or having fun?” Validate the need before talking about choices. Optional: ask each student to add one more kind way to the table.
