Part 3 · How can I protect others and myself?

If someone hurts me or my friend

1

Say “Stop!”

Stand tall. Use a strong voice.

→

2

Walk away

Go to friends or a safe place.

→

3

Tell an adult

Keep telling until someone helps.

Adults who can help:

Form teacher

School counsellor

Any teacher

Mum, Dad or family

Telling is not “tattling”. Telling keeps people safe.

About 4 minutes. Protecting MYSELF. Practise saying “Stop!” in a strong voice while standing tall: most children’s first try is too soft, so let them try 2 to 3 times. Explain the difference: tattling is to get someone into trouble, telling is to keep someone safe. Ask each student to name ONE adult in school they would tell. Remind them that your door is open: “If anyone ever hurts you, you can come and tell me too.” This matters because children who hurt others are sometimes being hurt as well.
