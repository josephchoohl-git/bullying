Part 3 · How can I protect others and myself?

When I feel angry or want to tease

Calm my body first. Then I can choose well.

Breathe

Smell the flower, blow out the candle. Do it 3 times.

Count

Count slowly from 1 to 10 in my head.

Move away

Walk away for a short while.

Use words

Say, “I feel ___ when ___.”

About 3 minutes. Practise “smell the flower, blow out the candle” together, three slow breaths. Explain simply: when we are angry or excited, our body goes fast and it is harder to think. Calming the body first makes “Stop, Think, Choose” possible. Ask each student: “Which one would work best for you?” Ask them to show you their choice so they rehearse it. If either student often gets angry, this can link to follow-up individual sessions.
