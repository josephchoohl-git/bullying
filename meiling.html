Part 2 · How does bullying affect me and others?

How does Mei Ling feel?

In her heart

Sad, scared, alone and embarrassed

In her body

Tummy aches, trouble sleeping, always tired

At school

Does not want to come to school. Stops drawing at recess.

Hurtful words and actions can hurt on the inside, even when no one can see it.

About 3 minutes. Before showing the cards, ask: “If you were Mei Ling, how would you feel?” Let them name feelings first, then reveal the cards. Then ask: “Has anyone ever made you feel a little like Mei Ling?” This builds empathy from their own experience. Many children who act hurtfully have also been hurt. If either student shares that they are being hurt by others, thank them and follow up one-to-one after the session.
