Part 3 · How can I protect others and myself?

I can be a helper

Include

“Come and play with us.”

Speak up

“Stop, that’s not kind.”

Stay close

Walk away together with the person.

Tell

Tell a teacher or the school counsellor.

If I hurt someone, I can make it right

Say sorry, give back what I took, and do something kind for them.

About 4 minutes. Protecting OTHERS. Turn the students into helpers: ask them to act out “Include” and “Speak up” with you (you play a child being left out). Rehearsal makes the new behaviour easier to choose at recess. The orange note is especially important for this group: it gives them a respectful way to repair, the same as Ben did. Ask: “Is there anyone you might want to make things right with?” Do not push for names. If they offer one, you can help them plan it after the session.
