About 3 minutes. Close with a simple Reality Therapy plan (the P in WDEP): small, specific, within their control, and something they can START doing rather than stop doing. Examples: “invite someone to play at recess”, “say one kind thing to a classmate every day”, “use Stop, Think, Choose when I want to tease”. Ask each student to say their plan aloud, or write it on paper to keep. Agree on a short check-in next week: “I’ll come and ask how your kind choice went.” End by thanking them for being honest and helpful today.
