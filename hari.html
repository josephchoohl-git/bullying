Part 2 · How does bullying affect me and others?

How does Hari feel?

Hari saw everything. He did not know what to do.

Worried

“Will this happen to me next?”

Unhappy

“Recess is not fun anymore.”

Sorry

“I wish I had helped her.”

Bullying makes the whole class feel less safe, not just one person.

About 2 minutes. Ask: “Hari didn’t do anything to Mei Ling. Why does he still feel bad?” Help them see that watching hurtful things makes the whole class feel less safe and less fun. Watchers are usually the biggest group, so they have the most power to change things. We will come back to how a watcher can become a helper in Part 3.
