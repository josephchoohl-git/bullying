Part 3 · How can I protect others and myself?

Stop. Think. Choose.

Stop

Pause before I say or do something.

hand up like a stop sign

Think

How will the other person feel?

finger on my head

Choose

Pick the kind way.

thumbs up

About 3 minutes. Teach the chant with actions and practise it three times, getting louder. STOP: hand up like a stop sign. THINK: finger on the side of the head. CHOOSE: thumbs up. This is the one thing to remember from today. It fits Choice Theory: we always have a choice about what we do. Ask: “When would Ben have needed Stop, Think, Choose?” (On Monday, before he said “baby drawing”.) Then: “When might YOU use it?” Keep it about the future, not past mistakes.
