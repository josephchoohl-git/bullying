Part 1 · What is bullying?

Four kinds of hurtful actions

Body

Pushing, hitting, grabbing or hiding someone’s things

Words

Mean names, laughing at someone, unkind comments

Leaving out

“Don’t play with her.” Making someone feel alone on purpose

Online

Mean messages, or sharing photos and chats to make fun of someone

About 3 minutes. Ask: “Which kinds did Ben use in his week?” (Words on Monday, body on Tuesday when he grabbed the drawing book, leaving out on Wednesday.) Point out that leaving someone out and unkind words hurt as much as hitting, even though no one can see the hurt. Keep the online card light for P3: many already use class chats or games, so name it simply and move on.
